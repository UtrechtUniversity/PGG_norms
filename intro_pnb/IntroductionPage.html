Game overview

  • At the beginning of each round, every player receives {{ C.endowment }} points.
  • Each player decides how many points to contribute to the group account (blue arrows in the example below) and how many to keep.
  • All contributions are added together (60 points in the example below).
  • The group account generates returns that are shared equally among all group members.
