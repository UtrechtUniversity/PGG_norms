Introduction

Welcome to this game! Thank you for participating. Please read these instructions carefully. You will receive a fixed amount of X euros for your participation, but you can earn more depending on your choices during the game. During the game, points will be used, which will later be converted into euros (X points = 1 euro). At the end of the game, you will be paid as soon as possible. Your final amount will be rounded to the nearest half euro.

Game Overview

This is an economic game. The game consists of {{rounds}} rounds. You will play with a group of {{players_per_group}} people, including yourself. Each round you will play with the same players. Your identities will remain anonymous.

Each game round consists of two phases. In the first phase, you decide how much to contribute to a fund (a common account). In the second phase, you will see an overview of the choices and earnings of all group members.

Phases per Game Round

  1. Phase 1: Contributing to the Fund

    At the beginning of each round, your group members and you each receive {{ C.endowment }} points. You decide how many of these points you want to contribute to a fund, whose proceeds are distributed among all group members. The remaining points you keep for yourself.

    For each point contributed to the fund, everyone receives {{C.efficiency_factor}} points, regardless of their own contribution. For each point that players keep for themselves, only they receive 1 point.

    The earnings of you and your fellow players per round are therefore:

    1 x (20 - own contribution to the fund) + {{ C.efficiency_factor }} x (group contribution to the fund)

    The players' earnings are thus maximized when everyone contributes the full amount of {{C.endowment}} points to the fund.

  2. Phase 2: Group Overview

    After all group members have decided how much to contribute to the fund, you will see the choices and earnings of your group members, and they will see yours.

Time per Round

In each round, you will have X seconds to enter your contribution to the fund, followed by X seconds to view the group's choices and earnings. So pay close attention! If you do not act within this time, the game will end for you!

If you have carefully read the instructions, you can start the game by clicking 'Next'.
